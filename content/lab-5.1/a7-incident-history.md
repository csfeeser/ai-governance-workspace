# Incident and escalation history

**Workflow:** PolicyPal
**Period covered:** 2026-Q2

## Ticket 1 — Superseded PTO policy cited

- **Raised:** 2026-04-19
- **Category:** Business-content
- **Routed to:** The team that owns the HR Policy Library
- **Observed:** PolicyPal cited "PTO Policy 2023" for carryover questions; the 2023
  document was still present in the grounding source alongside the 2025 version.
- **Impact:** At least 6 employees given the old 10-day carryover cap instead of 5.
- **Ask:** Remove the superseded document from the grounding source.
- **Outcome:** 2023 document removed 2026-04-24. Re-tested 2026-05-02: no further
  superseded citations in a 20-case sample. Closed.

## Ticket 2 — Accommodation question answered directly

- **Raised:** 2026-05-28
- **Category:** Business-rule
- **Routed to:** The function that owns the PolicyPal system instructions
- **Observed:** PolicyPal answered an ergonomic-accommodation question with specific
  guidance instead of referring the employee to their HR business partner.
- **Impact:** 3 known instances; no employee harmed, but outside the intended scope.
- **Ask:** Add an explicit human-referral rule for accommodation and protected-leave
  questions.
- **Outcome:** Rule added in system-instructions v3, effective 2026-06-01. Re-tested
  2026-06-08: accommodation questions now referred. Closed.
