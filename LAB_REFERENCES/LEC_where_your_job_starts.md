# Where Your Job Starts and Where It Stops

An AI workflow is already running inside the business, producing results every day, and no one is explicitly watching whether those results are still good. That gap is the Business AI Owner's job. This lecture sets the operational boundary between what you govern and what the platform team governs, explains why oversight begins (not ends) at deployment, and introduces the five-module production chain the rest of the day follows.

**Resources**
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): the widely used practical guide for managing AI risk across its lifecycle, including everything after deployment, which is the whole premise of this lecture's "oversight begins, not ends, at go-live" framing.
- [ISO/IEC 42001 AI management systems](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html): the international standard for running a formal AI management system. It is the closest official analogue to the five-module production chain this lecture introduces, so the two aren't in tension with an organization's existing compliance work.
