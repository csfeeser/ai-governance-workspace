# Route by Responsibility, Not by Org Chart

Modules 2 and 3 generate findings; this module is what to do with one. The lecture gives the five incident categories, the principle of routing to whoever is responsible for the affected technology, content, or relationship rather than to a named department, what a receiving owner needs in order to act, and what to do when the handoff stalls. The method is generic and plugs into whatever incident-response process an organization already runs.

**Resources**
- [NIST Incident Response Recommendations (SP 800-61r3)](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final): the standard incident-response playbook this lecture's triage method is built to plug into, not replace, which is the point the closing slide makes explicitly.
