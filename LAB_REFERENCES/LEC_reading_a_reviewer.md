# Reading a Reviewer, Not Just a Review

Human review of AI decisions is a control, and controls need monitoring. This lecture defines the three oversight metrics (agreement rate, override percentage, and disagreement trend) and the reviewer patterns that warrant a closer look, before students calculate them in Lab 3.1. Its central idea: the same override number can mean opposite things depending on what the AI was doing, which is why this module follows the quality audit.

**Resources**
- [EU AI Act Article 14: human oversight](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/14/): the regulatory version of this lecture's central claim, that a human sign-off only counts as oversight if it is substantive, not just present.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): its human-AI teaming guidance covers the same reviewer-monitoring practices this lecture turns into the three metrics you calculate in Lab 3.1.
