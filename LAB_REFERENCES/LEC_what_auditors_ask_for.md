# What Auditors Ask For and Why the Platform Won't Have It

Internal audit, risk teams, and regulators want the record that oversight happened, not the policy that says it should. This lecture covers the six evidence-packet components as answers to an auditor's questions, the roughly 180-day retention window on Copilot and Claude Enterprise logs, and where a multi-year retention requirement actually comes from. The regulatory content on this slide set is time-sensitive and is re-verified against current sources before each delivery.

**Resources**
- [EU AI Act Article 19: automatically generated logs](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/19/): the primary legal text for the six-month log-retention floor this lecture states for providers. Re-check it before each delivery rather than trusting the slide notes alone.
- [EU AI Act Article 26: obligations of deployers](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/26/): the matching deployer-side floor, the article that actually binds a Business AI Owner, as opposed to Article 19's provider-side version.
- [EU AI Act implementation timeline](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/): the source for the Digital Omnibus delay dates this lecture cites for high-risk deployer obligations. This page is the one to re-verify before every delivery, since it is exactly the kind of date that moves.
